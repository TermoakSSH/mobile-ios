Termoak is a modern SSH client to manage your servers from your iPhone and iPad, with the same sessions, hosts and keys as on your computer.

SESSIONS THAT NEVER DROP
Open the terminal on the Termoak server and pick it up where you left it: switch devices, lose coverage or lock your iPhone, and when you come back everything is still there, with its history and its processes.

A COMPLETE TERMINAL
• SSH with passwords, keys (Ed25519, RSA, ECDSA) and reusable identities, and Telnet for older equipment
• Jump hosts, proxy, agent forwarding and keep-alive
• Extra keys bar (Esc, Ctrl, Tab, arrows…), cursor gestures and command suggestions
• Snippets: save commands and run them on one or many servers at once
• SFTP file browser with uploads, downloads and a text editor
• Port forwarding (tunnels)
• Session recording, themes and fonts, the latency in the toolbar and a confirmation before pasting several lines
• Automatic detection of each host's system, shown with its logo
• Import your hosts from an SSH config file, CSV or other SSH clients

IPAD AND KEYBOARDS
On iPad, Termoak works like the desktop app: tabs, a sidebar, your hosts as cards and split view with up to four terminals, typing in all of them at once if you want. Hardware keyboards and trackpads are fully supported: Ctrl, Option as Meta, function keys, shortcuts such as ⌘K, ⌘T and ⌘W, scrolling and selecting with the trackpad.

ENCRYPTED, SYNCED VAULT
Hosts, keys, identities and snippets encrypted and synced across all your devices. Secrets never travel in plain text. Organize them in vaults: a personal one, others shared with people or your team, with Editor or Use-only permissions (connect without seeing passwords).

SHARE YOUR TERMINAL
Invite a person, your team or create a link. By default they can only watch; they can ask for the keyboard and you decide when to give it and for how long.

AI THAT ASKS FIRST
Ask the AI to investigate a failure or prepare a deployment, from its own section or from the copilot next to your terminal. It works on its own and asks for your approval before running anything sensitive, showing the exact command, its risk and the changes to each file. Works with your own API keys.

SEVERAL ACCOUNTS
Use the official server termoak.com or your own Termoak server, even several at once, or keep everything on this device only, without an account.

SECURITY
Two-step verification, app lock with Face ID, encrypted secrets and open source (AGPL-3.0), so you can audit it or run your own server.

Free, no ads, no card required. Also available for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android.
